02 the look changes without a style being written

The script moves a paragraph by a class, hides another by the hidden attribute, sizes a canvas by its width and height attributes (the style sheet gives it the width of its box and the height that follows) and a text box by rows. And the one exception: it puts a node where a drawing has it, by assigning the four properties left, top, width and height of the element's style object (the CSSOM), which the style sheet has made absolutely positioned. Every engine: all take effect, and no violation, under a style-src that is one hash, with no unsafe-inline and no unsafe-hashes.
