05 naive: a press taken from the click

Naive: a press is taken from the click event. WebKit 26, for a touch that falls on an element which cannot be pressed (no tabindex, no role of a control) 14 pixels below one that can, gives the click to the one that can, and reports it at a point on that one: the press lands on another thing than was touched. (Here the thing that can be pressed lies inside the paragraph touched, as a button drawn in a line of text does.) Chromium 153 and Firefox 155 give the click to the element touched, where it was touched. A listener on the touched element itself does not change what WebKit does.
