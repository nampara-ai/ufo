04 where a text box was changed

Each change in the text box is reported as (at, removed, inserted): from where the caret was before, or where an input method began, to where the selection ends after; the report is checked against the text before and after, and where it does not account for the change the whole text is reported instead. Every engine: the reports add up to the text, whatever is typed, removed, pasted, cut or undone.
