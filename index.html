UFO envelope spike: phone test files

Three test files. Each is one web page that carries a document, its tools and its own reader; nothing else is loaded. See PHONE-TEST.md for what to try, with a screen reader too. None of it has been tried on a real device.

  • compose.ufx.html: the test document as exported.
  • compose-edited.ufx.html: the same after edits, saved by the page itself (in headless Chromium) for people working on it: with its working history.
  • compose-published.ufx.html: the same state, saved for readers: the working history withheld.

To keep a copy of a file rather than open it, press and hold the link and choose to download it.
